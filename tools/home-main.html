Surface Preparation

A premium coating can't fix a poorly prepped slab.

Oil, grease, old coatings, curing compounds and weak surface material all interfere with adhesion. Coat over them and the floor can fail early.

Depending on the floor and system, we mechanically grind the concrete to remove contaminants and open a surface profile the coating can grip. Cracks, pits and spalling are evaluated and repaired before anything goes down.

When comparing quotes, ask every contractor how they prep. It takes more time and equipment, and it is the foundation for everything that follows.

Moisture & concrete

A slab can look dry while vapor still moves through it, which can cause adhesion problems. We consider moisture before choosing a system.

Is epoxy slippery?

Any smooth floor can be slick when wet. We can add texture or aggregate for traction, balanced against how easy you want it to mop.

Caring for your floor

Sweep grit regularly, mop with a suitable cleaner, wipe spills promptly, and avoid dragging sharp or very heavy objects.

New & Existing Concrete

Old stains don't rule out a new floor.

Most existing slabs can be coated after proper evaluation, repair and prep. Previous coatings may need to come off, damaged areas may need repair, and contaminated spots may need extra work.

Every slab is different, so we assess what is there before recommending what goes on top.

Why Hire a Professional?

DIY kits skip the hard part.

Mistakes show up quickly, and fixing a failed floor often means grinding it off and starting over. Professional grinders and experience make the difference.

Ask a Question
How long does epoxy flooring last?

It depends on the system, prep, installation quality, traffic, environment and maintenance. A home garage and a busy industrial floor place very different demands on a coating.

Can you install epoxy over cracked concrete?

Many cracks can be repaired before coating. Slabs can keep moving, though, and future movement may show through a rigid coating. We evaluate damage during prep.

Can epoxy go over an old coating?

Sometimes. We check the existing coating's condition, adhesion and compatibility. It may need to be removed or mechanically abraded first.

Is epoxy flooring easy to clean?

Yes. Coated concrete is much easier to maintain than bare, porous concrete. Regular sweeping and mopping cover everyday cleaning.

Can I customize the color?

Yes. Depending on the system there are many solid colors, flake blends and metallic effects to choose from.

Is epoxy flooring just for garages?

Not at all. Resinous floors are used in homes, retail, offices, warehouses, manufacturing and industrial facilities.

Can epoxy be used outdoors in Arizona?

Some systems work outside, but product choice matters. Exterior floors face intense UV, heat and monsoon moisture, so we recommend UV-stable options like polyaspartic for those spaces.

How much does epoxy flooring cost in Avondale?

Price depends on square footage, slab condition, prep, repairs, coating system, decorative options and number of layers. An on-site evaluation gives you an accurate quote.
